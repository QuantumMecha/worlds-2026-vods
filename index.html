How the spoiler protection works: Riot's YouTube uploads are whole broadcast days (title, length and chapter list give away results), so this page plays them inside a locked player: YouTube's own buttons, title, "more videos" and end screens are covered, the seek bar is always the same length (80 min from where the game starts), and playback stops when the next game's draft begins. Missing games (series ended early, or VOD not up yet) look exactly like real ones until you click them. Keyboard: Space play/pause, ←/→ 10s, F fullscreen, M mute. Data refreshes from Leaguepedia each time the page opens (only VOD links and team names are read — never scores).
